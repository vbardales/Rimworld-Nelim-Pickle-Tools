# Ancient Chinese Beast And Gene Expanded Renew

Updated 2026-09-24 by the mod's own session. This suite has thirteen feature files (52 scenarios once the
outlines are expanded) and five local step classes in `Tests/Pickle/Source/`. The current source and feature
counts are in `INVENTORY.md`, which was not regenerated by this note. Source presence only, not a runtime
pass: the suite has never been run.

| File | What to look for |
|---|---|
| `Stage.cs` | Helpers only: the current map, a pawn found by defName at a cell, "is an ordinary beast present", and the scenario-state records read through `ctx.Get<T>()` |
| `CriticalHookSteps.cs` | Drought teardown, the human sexie's scorpion, the qiongqi's landing, incident execution, the crow, private debug-action invocation by method name, recipe products through the real `GenRecipe` hook |
| `NianFirecrackerSteps.cs` | A real blow through `Thing.TakeDamage` on a chosen pawn and the damage it dealt; an ability activated on a target; butchery products written into the report; a firecracker explosion from its projectile's own def; a per-tick watch of short-lived pieces |
| `SchedulerSteps.cs` | Moving the game clock to just before a tick multiple or a local hour of a new year (the arithmetic is checked against `GenDate` at the tick it picks); a direct call of a scheduler under a `Rand` seed found to win a chance; switching the storyteller; triggering a `DebugActionYielder` node by its Keyed key |
| `BeastStateSteps.cs` | Living counts, "a thing of this def exists", a spawner in the richest room by the `Wealth` room stat, and attaching the logged errors to the report |
| `IncompatibilitySteps.cs` | Which running mod contains a def and which copy the game kept when two mods define it |

Worth reusing, adapted: the **per-tick watch** (`WatchPieces`), for anything that lives a few ticks and that a
`WaitUntil` poll between steps would miss; the **clock steps**, for any mod whose behaviour is scheduled by
`TicksGame` or by the local date; and `Tests/Pickle/Check-Steps.ps1`, which is stricter than the sibling
checkers: it fails on a feature line that matches no expression, on an ambiguous one, and on an `async` step with
no `TimeoutSeconds` (the default deadline is five seconds).

Facts found while writing it, not yet confirmed by a run: `RunSession.RegisterBuiltInEngineSteps` registers
`I save and reload`, `I save and reload as {string}` and `the save round trips` as string literals rather than
attributes, so an extraction of Pickle's step attributes cannot see them.

The suite references the mod's own assembly (`Private=false`) so that a scheduler member that goes away breaks
its build instead of its run. Inspect a step and its helpers before treating it as a generic extraction
candidate.
