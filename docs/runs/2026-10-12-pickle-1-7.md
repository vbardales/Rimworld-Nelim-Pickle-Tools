**What it proves.** On 1.7: the `net10.0` DLLs load; Pickle scans them (`Expansions: 1 steps dll(s)`); a step that reads the game
(expansions), one that clicks (KeyedClick) and one that reads a loaded colony (InspectTabs) pass.

**What it does not prove.**
- Rimmsqol has no 1.7 build (it needs a 1.7 `RIMMSqol.dll`): not staged. ColonistRace and VefFactions need third-party mods:
  a separate ticket later. So (a) covers 11 DLLs of 14, and only three of them have a scenario that uses them.
- The other tools (ClearScreen, HoverSteps, ClickDiagnostics, FilmTicks, ScreenshotMode, TextureOwner) loaded in (a) but no
  scenario of theirs was played on 1.7.
- The `path:` outside the repository worked in these runs; it stays a local arrangement.
- No No Version Warning mod was staged (the copies declare 1.7).

**Log lines that are not defects.**
- `Mod <tool> did not load any content`, 13 times in (a): these mods ship no Defs, only Pickle assemblies. The same message is in
  the 1.6 logs (`evidence/aggregate/2026-09-23-v4.8.4-fixed-minimal-en/Player.log`).
- `GLFW Error (PlatformUnavailable): Wayland: Platform not initialized` in (c): the game falls back to X11 under Xvfb.
